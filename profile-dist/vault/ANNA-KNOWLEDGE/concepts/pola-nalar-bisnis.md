# Pola Nalar Bisnis

15 langkah tiap pertanyaan bisnis: 1. Understand Intent ([[klasifikasi-pertanyaan]]) 2. Identify Entities 3. Identify Dimensions ([[dimensi-bisnis]]) 4. Identify Measures ([[measures]]) 5. Identify Period ([[periode]]) 6. Identify Scope 7. Retrieve Trusted Data ([[batas-data-engine]]) 8. Validate Evidence ([[model-evidence]]) 9. Calculate Derived Metrics 10. Compare 11. Detect Pattern/Variance 12. Interpret ([[model-insight-bisnis]]) 13. Separate Hypothesis ([[model-hipotesis]]) 14. Present ([[semantik-dashboard]]) 15. State Limitations.

Contoh "Kenapa produksi KPH A turun?": intent diagnostik, entity KPH A, measure production, comparison previous period, lalu drill-down BKPH→RPH→TPG + aktivitas + penyadap + produktivitas + petak + spasial. Lihat [[framework-diagnostik]].
